# Support Page Draft

## Kana Sprint Support

Kana Sprint is a Japanese learning game for kana, JLPT N5-N1 vocabulary, and original anime-style practice lines.

For help, bug reports, or App Store review questions, contact the developer through your published support email address.

## Common Questions

### Does the app require an account?

No. Kana Sprint does not require sign-in, account creation, or cloud sync.

### Where is progress stored?

Progress, high scores, daily streaks, daily goal progress, settings, and learned-item counts are stored locally on the device. Use Settings > Local data > Reset progress to clear local progress.

### Why are ads unavailable?

The continue-run rewarded ad only appears when production AdMob app IDs and rewarded ad-unit IDs are configured for the release build.

### Does the app quote real anime?

No. The anime-style Japanese lines are original study material and do not quote protected anime scripts, characters, brands, or titles.
